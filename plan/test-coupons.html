Deferred: Test coupons before a long print

Idea

When a fit is critical and the print is expensive/long, generate a small auxiliary plate first: a few holes at different diameters, a few slots at different clearances, a fragment of a snap-fit or thread, or a MagSafe-pocket sample — print that first, measure, then commit the full model's clearance parameter.

Why deferred

references/engineering-checklist.md §1 already states "test-fit coupons are mandatory for expensive or long prints" as a principle. What's missing is only the mechanical step of auto-generating a coupon plate geometry — genuinely useful, but it's a process nudge more than a code gap, and building a coupon generator before it's asked for is speculative.

When to build it

Next time a critical-fit part is requested for a long/expensive print — generate the coupon manually that time, and only then turn the pattern into a reusable macro/script if it recurs.

What it would touch

  • A new small FreeCAD macro or extension of assets/freecad-parametric-template.FCMacro that emits a coupon plate from a list of trial clearance values in parameters.json.
  • SKILL.md — add an optional step between drawing approval and full 3D build for critical-fit tasks.
